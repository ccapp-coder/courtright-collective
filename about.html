About

A family-run studio for the small businesses and indie founders we'd want to work with.

Courtright Collective is an independent studio in Nashville, Tennessee. We build Tealden, our done-for-you video service, plus the problem-first business tools that will live together as Belnoda. And we still ship indie apps with founders ready to launch.

What we believe

Most small businesses don't need a full-time hire, a $400-an-hour consultant, or another SaaS tool with twelve features and no answer. They need one good thing built well, deployed cleanly, and maintained when their business changes. Sometimes that's a booking page. Sometimes that's a website. Sometimes that's the video content you keep saying you'll get to next weekend.

That's the whole job. We're a small team that handles the build and the upkeep. You describe the outcome; we figure out how to get there.

What I bring to this

Before Courtright Collective there was the rest of my life. Four threads pull together into how I do this work.

Ranch work. Taught me what hard work actually is. Not the performance of hard work, the thing itself. Cows don't care if you're tired.

Touring in bands. Taught me how to serve a room. Reading the energy. Knowing when to push and when to ease back. A bad night is data, not a verdict.

Bartending. Taught me that the best service feels like attention paid, not effort spent. Remembering what someone drinks. Knowing when to talk and when not to. Making the place a regular comes back to.

Six years brokering freight. Taught me how to map a business on a phone call. What do they actually need. What's slowing them down. What would make their week easier without them having to ask.

All four show up in this work every day. Whether I'm shipping a new tool, scoping a website, tuning someone's daily video, or talking through an app idea with a founder. Same playbook every time: pay close attention, work hard, make someone's life easier.

The best service feels like attention paid, not effort spent.

How we got here

I started Courtright Collective as an indie app studio. That's how TinkerTaps and Trivd came to be. Building apps is creative, slow work, and the wins are far apart.

Somewhere along the way I noticed the pattern in what small-business owners kept asking for. It was never "build me an app." It was "make this one headache go away." The bookings, the follow-ups, the customer list scattered across three inboxes, the content they never had time to make. So we started building for the headaches instead of the categories.

The flagship of that shift is Tealden, our done-for-you video service. You tell it about your business once, and a fresh branded video shows up every day without you opening an editor. The tools behind the other headaches are becoming rooms in one house we call Belnoda: one login, one customer list, turn on only what you need. It's coming soon, and the rooms are already open.

The app side still exists too, under our partnership program for founders ready to ship. Same studio, same family, one set of values.
