## Internal notes, delete before sending

**Total time: 45 minute call plus 30 to 40 minutes writing it up. Send within 24 hours, always.**

### Before the call, 10 minutes

- [ ] Look at their website on a phone
- [ ] Search their business name and their main service plus city
- [ ] Look at the Google Business Profile
- [ ] Check their most active social account
- [ ] Note three things that are working. You will need them

### The call, 45 minutes

- **0 to 5:** How is business? Let them talk. Do not open with your findings
- **5 to 15:** Where do customers come from today? What do you wish happened more? What have you tried?
- **15 to 25:** Your observations. **Lead with what is working.** Then one honest thing that is not
- **25 to 40:** Build the plan together, out loud. Ask "could you actually do that?" on every item. If the answer is no, the item is wrong
- **40 to 45:** Recap the three things for this week. Tell them the written plan lands within 24 hours

### Rules

- **Talk less than half the time.** If you talked more than they did, you sold instead of listened
- Never say the word "just" before a task. Nothing is "just" anything to somebody who has not done it
- Every item gets a who and a time estimate. A plan without those is a wish list
- The "what to ignore" section is what makes people trust the rest of it
- Send the write-up within 24 hours. The value of this call decays fast and so does the goodwill
- If the honest answer is that they do not need you, say so and refund it. That person tells more people about you than a client would
