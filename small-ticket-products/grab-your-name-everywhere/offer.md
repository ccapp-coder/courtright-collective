## Sales blurb

**Most people do this backwards and then live with it forever.**

They set up Instagram first, get attached to the handle, then find out the `.com` is gone. Now their website and their social accounts do not match, and they never will.

Three hours, in the right order, once.

**The order that protects you:**

0. **Check everything, claim nothing.** Ten minutes with a notepad, including the free USPTO search that stops you building on top of somebody else's registered mark.
1. **Domain first.** The only asset in this entire list you actually own. Everything else is borrowed from a company that could change its rules on a Tuesday.
2. **Email on your domain.** The cheapest credibility upgrade available, plus the SPF, DKIM, and DMARC setup that stops your mail landing in spam. That is a problem you will not know you have.
3. **Google Business Profile.** If you serve local customers this is worth more than every social account combined, and it is free. Full 15-point setup including the primary category, which affects your local ranking more than anything else on the profile.
4. **Social handles.** All of them, even the ones you will never post on. A dormant account you own beats an active account somebody else owns with your name on it.
5. **The directories that actually matter,** sorted by industry so you can skip the ones that do not apply to you without guilt. Including Apple Business Connect, which feeds Apple Maps and which almost nobody claims.
6. **Lock it down.** Password manager, two-factor on the two master keys, and a second admin on Google and Facebook, because a sole-admin account is one lost phone from a disaster.
7. **Trademark, the honest version.** What it actually costs, when it is worth it, and what to do today for free.

**Plus a 49-row CSV checklist** with priority and who-needs-it on every line, because you will not finish in one sitting and you will absolutely forget which platform you were on.

**$19. One afternoon. Done for years.**

Auto-renew plus a calendar reminder prevents it completely and takes two minutes. It is step 1, item 3.
