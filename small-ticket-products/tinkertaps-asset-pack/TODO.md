# TinkerTaps Art and Sound Asset Pack

**Status: not built. This one needs Dillon.**

Every other product in this library is finished and sellable. This one is not, and it cannot be, because it requires original art and audio that has to be made rather than written.

Skipping it was the right call rather than shipping placeholder files that would need to be thrown away.

---

## What this product is meant to be

A downloadable pack of toddler-safe art and sound assets, sold to other indie developers and to parents making things for their kids. It would be the first TinkerTaps product that sells to a second audience.

**Suggested price:** $49 for the standard pack, $149 for a commercial license tier.

**Feeds into:** the partnership program and Application Creation, since the buyer is another indie developer building something for kids.

---

## What Dillon needs to make

### Art

- [ ] **40 to 60 illustrated objects,** the TinkerTaps visual style. Animals, food, vehicles, shapes, household things. PNG with transparency, at 1x, 2x, and 3x
- [ ] **8 to 12 background scenes.** Simple, low-detail, low-contrast, nothing that competes with the foreground
- [ ] **A UI kit:** buttons, a progress indicator, a parent-gate frame, a back arrow, a settings gear. All at toddler tap-target sizes, which is much larger than adult UI
- [ ] **6 to 8 particle and reward graphics.** Stars, sparkles, confetti. Gentle ones, nothing strobing
- [ ] **A color palette file** with the TinkerTaps palette as hex, plus swatch files for the common design tools
- [ ] **Source files,** not just exports. Buyers of an asset pack expect to be able to recolor things

### Sound

- [ ] **20 to 30 interaction sounds.** Taps, pops, chimes, swishes. Soft attack, short, nothing startling
- [ ] **8 to 12 success and reward sounds,** in a consistent musical key so they can be layered without clashing
- [ ] **3 to 5 ambient loops,** two to three minutes each, seamless, quiet enough to sit under a voice
- [ ] **A small set of transition sounds** for screen changes
- [ ] **Format:** WAV at 48kHz for the source, plus compressed versions for shipping
- [ ] **Loudness normalized and peak limited.** Toddlers hold devices close to their faces. This matters more here than in any other kind of app

### Documentation, which can be written once the assets exist

- [ ] A README with the file structure, naming convention, and formats
- [ ] A license file. What buyers can and cannot do. Use in commercial apps yes, resell the pack itself no
- [ ] A one-page style guide so anybody extending the pack stays consistent
- [ ] A short "how to use these well with toddlers" note, which is the thing that would genuinely differentiate this pack from a generic asset bundle

---

## The two decisions to make before starting

1. **Licensing model.** Royalty-free with attribution, royalty-free without, or tiered by company size. The simplest that works: one price, commercial use allowed, no reselling the pack itself, no attribution required.

2. **Whether these are the actual TinkerTaps assets or a parallel set.** Selling the real ones means competitors can ship something that looks like TinkerTaps. A parallel set in the same style costs more work but keeps the app's identity. Recommendation: a parallel set, drawn in the same style, no overlap with the shipping app.

## Realistic effort

| Piece | Rough time |
| --- | --- |
| Art, 60 objects plus scenes plus UI | 25 to 40 hours |
| Sound, 40 to 50 clips plus loops | 10 to 15 hours |
| Documentation and packaging | 4 hours |
| Store page and listing copy | 2 hours |

**Call it 40 to 60 hours.** Which is a real project, not an afternoon, and it is worth deciding whether that time is better spent on a TinkerTaps content update that grows the app itself.

---

## Recommendation

**Park it.** The rest of this library is finished and can start earning immediately. This one is 40 to 60 hours of specialist work for a narrow audience.

Two things to check first:

1. Does anybody actually ask for it? Put a "coming soon, tell me if you want this" form on the TinkerTaps site and count.
2. Would those same 40 hours grow TinkerTaps itself more than the pack would earn?

If the answers come back the right way, build the sound half first. It is a third of the effort, it is the half that is genuinely hard to source elsewhere for toddler apps, and it can ship on its own at $29 to test the market.
