## Sales blurb

**Most people use AI like a search engine and then decide AI is overrated.**

Six words in, something generic out, back to doing it by hand. I did the exact same thing for about a month before it clicked.

Here's the thing: the model is not the problem. The prompt is. A good prompt gives the AI a job title, hands over your real mess, says exactly what shape the answer should take, and sets the bar for what good means. Miss any one of those and you get filler.

The Founder's AI Prompt Vault is 70 prompts that do all four. Written for people running small businesses, not for prompt engineers. Paste one in, swap the brackets for your real details, get something you can actually use.

**What is inside, 9 categories:**

- **Marketing and copy.** Homepage headlines, About page rewrites, a week of social posts from one idea, ad angles, mining your customers' own words
- **Sales and follow-up.** The follow-up nobody hates, call notes to proposal, an objection stress test that will sting a little, the price increase letter
- **Customer service.** The angry customer reply, saying no without burning the bridge, building your FAQ out of your own inbox
- **Operations.** Brain dump to written SOP, the weekly reset, finding what you should actually automate
- **Money.** Explain my own numbers to me, should I raise prices, the pricing tier builder, a 13-week cash flow check
- **Hiring and delegating.** Job posts real people answer, a 30-day onboarding plan, the delegation brief
- **Product and offers.** Pressure test my idea (it will tell you if it is bad), the smallest sellable version, naming, onboarding sequences
- **Content.** Finding your angles, getting interviewed into a blog post, repurposing one long thing into ten short ones
- **The meta prompts.** Make my prompt better, critique your own answer, learn my voice, and the reality check that separates what the AI knows from what it is guessing

Plus the universal prompt formula for when you need one that is not in the vault, and one honest warning about where AI will confidently lie to you.
