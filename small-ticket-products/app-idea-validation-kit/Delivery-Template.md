## Internal notes, delete before sending

**Time budget: 3 to 4 hours.**

- 30 min: intake form review, write out the six scoping questions
- 45 min: store research, both stores, five competitors minimum
- 60 min: read one and two star reviews. This is the section people pay for. Do not rush it
- 30 min: community research
- 30 min: scoping, pricing arithmetic, and the two-week test
- 45 min: write it up

**Rules:**

- **Be willing to say no.** A validation kit that always says build it is worth nothing and word gets around. Roughly one in three should be a no or a "build something narrower"
- Quote reviews verbatim. Never paraphrase. The verbatim quotes are the single most valuable thing in the document
- Do the revenue arithmetic even when it is grim. Especially when it is grim
- Kill criteria must be numbers, not feelings
- If it is a no, still give them the smallest thing worth doing instead
- Never promise store performance, download numbers, or revenue. Ranges and reasoning only
