## Notes and assumptions

- **Priced at $149 and deliberately close to break-even.** This is a sales tool, not a profit center. Five hours of build time at $149 is not a business, but the conversion rate from "clicked a portal with my own logo on it" to a four-figure build engagement is high enough to make it the best-spending five hours in the studio.
- **Scope protection is the whole operational challenge.** The internal notes cap it at five hours, three to five screens, and mandate starting from the Indie App Starter Kit scaffold rather than from zero. After three of these there is a reusable component library and the build time roughly halves.
- **Being explicit about what is faked is non-negotiable.** It appears twice in the deliverable, once as a bold statement up front and once as a per-screen table. Overselling a demo is the single most reliable way to poison the build engagement that follows it.
- **The honest assessment section is required,** same as in the other services. Sometimes the answer really is a shared folder, and being the person who says so is what makes every other recommendation credible.
- **This is the strongest product for the law firm vertical** alongside the Legal Intake Checklist. Solo attorneys and small firms feel the document-chasing problem acutely and have the budget to fix it.
- Demo comes down at 30 days, with a calendar reminder in the checklist. Leaving old client demos deployed forever is how a studio ends up with fifteen forgotten subdomains.
- Best upsell path: Demo, then the Starter tier build, then a maintenance retainer. The three tiers are priced in the deliverable so the next conversation is a decision rather than a negotiation.
