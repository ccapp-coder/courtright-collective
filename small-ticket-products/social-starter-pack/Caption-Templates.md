# 90 Caption Templates

Fill the brackets with specifics, not adjectives. `[result]` is never "great results," it is "cut their power bill by a third" or "three fewer no-shows a week."

**Two rules for every one of these.** First line has to earn the second. And cut a third of whatever you wrote.

---

## Pillar 1: Teach (40 percent of your posts)

### Myth and correction

1. `[Common belief]` is the most expensive myth in `[industry]`. Here is what is actually true: `[the truth]`.
2. Three things people get wrong about `[topic]`: 1) `[wrong]`, 2) `[wrong]`, 3) `[wrong]`. The last one costs the most.
3. You do not need `[expensive thing everyone recommends]`. You need `[the actual thing]`. Here is why.
4. "`[Thing people say]`" Not really. `[The correction, in one sentence.]`
5. Everybody says `[common advice]`. It is fine advice. It is also why `[bad outcome]` keeps happening.

### Straight answers

6. The question I get most: "`[question]`" The honest answer: `[answer]`.
7. What `[service]` actually costs, and what moves the price: `[factor]`, `[factor]`, `[factor]`.
8. How to tell if you need `[service]`: if `[sign]`, `[sign]`, or `[sign]`, it is time. If not, save your money.
9. `[Thing]` or `[other thing]`? Here is how I decide: `[the deciding factor]`.
10. Somebody asked me `[question]` today. Answering it here because I get it every week.

### Lists that get saved

11. `[Number]` things to check before you `[action]`: `[list]`. Save this one.
12. The `[number]`-point checklist we run on every `[job type]`: `[list]`.
13. `[Number]` signs it is time to `[action]`: `[list]`.
14. Everything you need for `[task]`, and nothing you do not: `[list]`.
15. `[Number]` questions to ask before you hire anybody for `[service]`. Including us.

### Teaching from mistakes

16. Mistake I see every single week: `[mistake]`. Costs about `[cost]` to fix. Takes `[time]` to prevent.
17. The most expensive shortcut in `[industry]`: `[shortcut]`. Here is what it actually saves and what it costs.
18. Got this one wrong for years: `[the wrong approach]`. What changed my mind: `[the thing]`.
19. If you only fix one thing about your `[thing]` this year, make it `[the thing]`.
20. This is what `[problem]` looks like before it becomes `[expensive problem]`. Catch it here.

### Explaining the work

21. What actually happens during `[service]`, start to finish: `[steps]`.
22. Why `[thing]` takes `[time]` and not `[shorter time]`. It is `[the reason]`.
23. The part of this job nobody sees: `[the invisible work]`. It is most of it.
24. `[Term people hear but do not understand]`, explained in plain language: `[explanation]`.
25. Two ways to do `[task]`. The fast one costs `[cost]` and lasts `[time]`. The right one costs `[cost]` and lasts `[time]`.

### Seasonal teaching

26. Every `[season]` we get the same call: `[the problem]`. Here is how to avoid being that call.
27. Before `[seasonal event]`, check these `[number]` things: `[list]`.
28. `[Month]` is when `[problem]` shows up. Here is the ten-minute version of preventing it.

## Pillar 2: Show (30 percent)

29. `[Time]` this morning. `[What you are doing.]` Nobody sees this part, and it is the part that makes `[the visible result]` work.

### Process

35. Time-lapse of the boring part. It takes `[real duration]` in real life.
36. Watch what happens when `[technique]`. `[The result.]`
37. Same job, three angles. `[One line on what to look at.]`
38. Start to finish on `[job]`. `[Duration.]` `[One thing that surprised you.]`

### Before and after

39. Before. After. `[Time]` of work in between.

41. This is what `[number]` `[hours/days]` of `[work]` looks like.

### Tools and craft

43. The tool I would not work without: `[tool]`. `[Why.]`
44. Been using this `[tool]` for `[time]`. Here is what it replaced and why that mattered.
45. People ask what is in the truck. `[List the actually useful things.]`
46. Not the fanciest option. Just the one that has never let me down.

### Workspace and life

48. Twelve hours ago this was `[state]`. `[One line.]`
49. Some days it is `[glamorous thing]`. Today it is `[unglamorous thing]`. Both count.
50. `[Number]` years doing this and `[thing]` still gets me every time.

### The people

## Pillar 4: Ask (10 percent)

### The plain ask

70. We do `[service]` for `[who]` in `[area]`. If that is you, `[how to reach you]`.
71. Booking `[timeframe]` now. `[How to book.]`
72. Two openings left this `[week / month]`. `[How to grab one.]`
73. If you have been meaning to `[thing]`, this is your sign. `[How to start.]`

### Offers

74. `[Offer]` through `[date]`. `[What they get.]` `[How to claim.]`
75. `[Season]` special: `[offer]`. `[One line on why now is the right time.]`
76. New for `[month]`: `[the new thing]`. `[Who it is for.]`

### Soft asks

77. Quietly taking on `[number]` more `[clients / projects]` this `[quarter]`. If you know somebody, send them my way.
78. Not a sales post. Just a reminder that we exist and we are good at `[thing]`.
79. If `[problem]` has been on your list since `[time period]`, I promise it is not as bad as you think.

### Referral asks

80. Best compliment there is: sending somebody our way. Thank you to everyone who has.
81. Know somebody who needs `[service]`? We have room this `[period]`.

## Bonus: engagement posts

Use one every couple of weeks. Comments are the cheapest reach there is.

82. Honest question for `[audience]`: what is the one task you would pay to never do again? I will go first. `[Yours.]`
83. `[Option A]` or `[option B]`? I have a strong opinion and I am curious if I am alone.
84. What is the worst advice you have ever gotten about `[topic]`?
85. Fill in the blank: the hardest part of `[thing]` is ______.
86. Guess how long this took. Closest answer gets bragging rights.
87. What do you want to know about `[your field]`? Ask me anything, I will answer all of them.
88. Rate this `[before and after]` out of ten. Be honest.
89. Tell me you are a `[type of person]` without telling me you are a `[type of person]`.
90. What is a thing about `[your industry]` that you wish more people knew?

---

## The eleven-word rule

If the first line of your caption does not work as a standalone sentence that makes someone want the second line, rewrite it.

That is the only editing rule you need.
