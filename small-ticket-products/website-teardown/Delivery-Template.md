## Internal notes, delete before sending

**Time budget: 60 to 75 minutes total.**

- 10 min: cold pass on mobile. Screenshot everything before you learn anything about them
- 10 min: read every page, note the copy verbatim
- 10 min: contact paths, forms, trust signals
- 10 min: speed and mobile checks, run PageSpeed Insights
- 10 min: SEO basics, `site:` search, check the Google Business Profile matches
- 20 min: write it up

**Rules:**

- Never say "your site is bad." Say what a visitor experiences and what it costs
- Every criticism gets a specific fix. No exceptions
- Always include section 9. It is the one that gets you the reply
- Quote their copy exactly. People do not remember what their own site says
- If the honest answer is "this site is fine, spend your money on ads instead," say that. You will lose one project and gain somebody who tells people about you
