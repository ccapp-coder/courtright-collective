# Running the 30 Day Challenge

**Internal document. Do not send this to participants.**

Participant `[NAME]` | Cohort `[MONTH]` | Start date `[DATE]` | Ship date `[DATE + 30]`

---

## What the participant gets

- [ ] The 30-day outline as a PDF, on day 0
- [ ] The Indie App Starter Kit, full download
- [ ] The Launch Playbook
- [ ] The ASO Cheat Sheet
- [ ] Three group calls: kickoff, day 15, launch week
- [ ] A shared channel for questions, answered within one business day
- [ ] One 20-minute one-to-one, redeemable any time in the 30 days
- [ ] A ship-date accountability email at day 7, 14, 21, 28, and 30

## Day 0, before the cohort starts

- [ ] Welcome email with the outline PDF and the Starter Kit download
- [ ] Ship date stated as a real calendar date, not "in 30 days"
- [ ] Calendar invites for all three group calls
- [ ] Channel invite
- [ ] Intake form: what they want to build, their experience level, how many hours a week they honestly have
- [ ] **Read every intake form.** Anybody whose idea is obviously too big gets a personal note before day 1, not on day 5

## The kickoff call, 45 minutes

- **0 to 10:** Everybody says their idea in one sentence. Enforce the one sentence, kindly but actually
- **10 to 25:** The scoping questions, run live. Anybody scoring under 24 picks something smaller on the call
- **25 to 35:** How the thirty days work. The rules. Where people quit and why
- **35 to 45:** Questions

**The single most valuable thing you do all month happens in this call:** making people cut their idea down before they start. Everyone who fails this challenge fails because their day 1 idea was too big, not because their day 15 effort was too small.

## Where people quit, and what to do about it

| Day | What happens | Your move |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **3** | Reading bad reviews is boring. Some skip it | Post one great find from a competitor's reviews in the channel. Makes it feel worth doing |
| **5** | Cutting in half hurts | Post about something you cut from your own app that you never missed |
| **10** | The ugly core loop feels bad. This is the biggest drop-off point | Post an ugly screenshot of one of your own apps at day 10. Nothing helps more than this |
| **14** | Using your own app reveals problems. Motivation dips | Remind them: three fixes only, the rest goes in version two |
| **19** | Device testing finds real breakage | Offer the one-to-one slot here. Most people use it around now |
| **22 to 24** | Store listing and legal work is nobody's favorite | Templates. Just hand them templates |
| **26** | Submission anxiety | Remind everyone that most first submissions get rejected once and it is routine |
| **29** | Rejection lands | Same-day response in the channel. Rejection at day 29 is where somebody quits at the finish line |

## Accountability emails

**Day 7:** "Week one done. You should have a sentence, a cut list, sketches, and a name. Reply with your sentence."
**Day 14:** "Halfway. Core loop working and data sticking? If not, tell me where you are stuck today, not on day 25."
**Day 21:** "Nine days. Everything from here is store paperwork and polish. The hard part is behind you."
**Day 28:** "Two days. If you are not submitted, submit tomorrow with whatever you have. Manual release."
**Day 30:** "Ship it. Post the link in the channel. We will all download it."

Keep them short. Four lines maximum. Long check-in emails do not get read by somebody who is behind, which is exactly who they are for.

## The day 15 call, 30 minutes

- Everybody shares their screen for 60 seconds. **Enforce the timer**
- The main job is normalizing how rough everything looks at day 15
- Unblock anybody genuinely stuck
- Publicly name anybody who has cut scope. Reward the behavior you want

## Launch week call, 45 minutes

- Everybody demos
- Walk through the store submission and rejection process live
- Set the day 31 expectation clearly: launch day is quiet, and that is normal
- Ask each person what they are building next

## After the cohort

- [ ] Download and use every app that shipped. Leave an honest review on each
- [ ] Ask for a testimonial from anybody who shipped, in the week after they ship, while they are still elated
- [ ] Post the ship count publicly. It is the only marketing this challenge needs
- [ ] Personal note to anybody who did not ship, with no pitch attached, offering their spot in the next cohort free

## The metric that matters

**Ship rate.** Not signups, not satisfaction scores. What percentage of people who started had something live in a store on day 30.

Publish it honestly, including the misses. A challenge that says "9 of 12 shipped" is more credible and sells better than one implying everyone did.

## Pricing and capacity notes

- **$199 per person.** Cohorts of 8 to 12
- **Time cost per cohort:** roughly 3 hours of calls, plus 30 to 45 minutes a day in the channel for a month. Call it 20 hours
- At 10 people that is $1,990 for about 20 hours, which works, and the real return is that a third of participants become build clients or partnership candidates
- **Do not run these back to back.** One cohort a quarter, maximum. The value is in the attention, and attention does not scale
- Run the first one at a reduced price for a small group to find where the outline breaks. It will break somewhere around day 10 and day 22
