## Sales blurb

**Your app is fine. Nobody can find it.**

That was my problem for about four months. Good app, decent reviews, downloads flat as a table. I assumed I needed more marketing. What I actually needed was to stop wasting 40 of my 100 keyword characters on the word "app" and on plurals Apple already handled for me.

The ASO Cheat Sheet is every rule I learned the slow way, on one working sheet. No theory, no 60-page course, no "leverage the algorithm."

**What is on it:**

- **Every field and every character limit,** iOS and Android, in two tables. Which fields are searchable, which are not, and what each one is actually for.
- **The keyword rules people get wrong.** Apple builds phrase combinations for you, so multi-word phrases waste space. Never repeat a word across fields. No spaces after commas. The seven things to strike from your keyword field entirely.
- **The free localization trick** that is the closest thing to free downloads in ASO. Same English copy, extra keyword sets.
- **How to pick keywords** with a three-factor score, plus five free places to find real search terms before you pay a single dollar for a tool.
- **Fill-in formulas** for your app name, subtitle, and description, with a worked before-and-after on a keyword field that shows exactly what wasted space looks like.
- **Nine screenshot rules,** because screenshots do more conversion work than every word on your listing combined. Including the squint test.
- **Ratings and reviews.** When to fire the prompt (right after a win, never on launch), how many you get per year, and the review reply that turns two stars into four.
- **The signals table.** What actually moves rankings, ranked by weight, including the uncomfortable one nobody wants to hear about retention.
- **The testing loop.** How to run Product Page Optimization and Store Listing Experiments without fooling yourself.
- **A 30-day rhythm** and a **launch-day worksheet** you fill in before you hit submit.

**$19. Print it. Work off it. Submit with confidence.**

> Honest note: ASO gets people to your page. It cannot fix an app people leave on day two. If your retention is broken, better keywords just buy you more expensive churn. The sheet says so out loud, in the section where most guides would keep selling you.
