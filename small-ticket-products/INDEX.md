## What is in every folder

Each product folder contains:

- **The deliverable.** The actual files a customer downloads
- **`offer.md`.** Product name, price, format, what it feeds into, the sales blurb in Dillon's voice (this is the landing and checkout copy), and notes on any judgment calls made while building it

Service folders contain the offer plus a **delivery template**, which is the document Dillon fills in when somebody buys. Each of those ends with an internal notes block, clearly marked, that must be deleted before sending. Those blocks carry the time budget, the operating rules, and the traps.

## The tooling

`_tools/` holds what built the library:

- **`build_pdf.py`.** Markdown to branded PDF. Cover page with the Courtright palette, embedded Cormorant Garamond and Outfit, and three brand themes (Courtright, TinkerTaps, Trivd). Cover metadata lives in an HTML comment at the top of each Markdown file
- **`inject_fonts.py`.** Inlines the brand fonts as base64 into the self-contained HTML tools, so they stay on-brand with zero network calls
- **`brand-fonts.css`.** The two brand fonts, base64 encoded, latin subset
- **`BRAND.md`.** Palette, type, voice rules, and how to rebuild any PDF

Rebuild any PDF with:

```bash
cd small-ticket-products
python3 _tools/build_pdf.py <slug>/<file>.md <slug>/<file>.pdf
```

## What was verified rather than assumed

Three products contain working code, and all three were run rather than eyeballed:

- **The Indie App Starter Kit** was driven through every flow in a real browser: onboarding, routing, add, toggle, delete with confirm, persistence across reload, theme persistence, and service worker registration. Zero console errors on a fresh load. One real bug was found and fixed that way, where `display:grid` was overriding the `hidden` attribute.
- **The eBay Repricing Calculator** had every mode checked by hand: profit, break-even, target profit, target margin, and the loss case. All correct to the cent.
- **The QR Menu Maker** contains a QR encoder written from scratch. Seventy generated codes across both error correction levels, versions 1 through 10, and two render scales were decoded with zxing, the decoder family phone cameras use. All seventy read back byte for byte. Two real bugs were found and fixed during that testing: a swapped term in the Reed-Solomon generator polynomial, and format information bits placed in reverse order. Both would have produced codes that look completely convincing and scan on nothing.

The **Logo + Brand Starter's** contrast grading uses real WCAG 2 relative luminance and was checked against published values: black on white returns 21:1, and #777777 on white returns 4.48:1.

## House rules followed throughout

- **No em dashes.** Anywhere, in any file. Verified across all 112 files
- Dillon's voice on every piece of customer-facing copy. Fellow traveler, never guru
- Every product tells the buyer what it does not do, and who it is not for
- Every service is willing to say no, in writing, on the offer page
- No fabricated statistics, download numbers, or revenue figures anywhere
- No promised rankings, no promised results, no guaranteed outcomes
