## The offer page

### You are probably only found by people who already know your name.

That is the most common thing I find. The business ranks perfectly for its own name and nowhere at all for the thing it actually sells. Which means every customer who finds you had to already know you existed, which means you do not have a search problem, you have a referral business with a website attached.

**I will run the searches your customers actually run,** from your city, and write down where you show up and where you do not.

**What you get:**

- **Eight real searches,** run from your service area with the location set manually, because auditing from the wrong city produces confidently wrong results. Your name, your service plus "near me," your service plus your city, your neighborhood, the emergency variant, and the angle your competitors are winning on.
- **A full Google Business Profile review,** sixteen checks including the primary category, which is the single biggest ranking lever on the whole profile and which most businesses set once and never think about again.
- **A review comparison** against your three closest competitors: total, average, last 90 days, and reply rate. With a specific number for what it would take to be in the same conversation, and how many months of asking that actually is.
- **Name, address, and phone consistency** across your website and eight listings. Google decides whether you are one real business or three sketchy ones by comparing these. Fixing the mismatches is boring and it works.
- **Twelve website checks** from a local search angle, including the structured data and the tap-to-call link that a surprising number of sites are missing.
- **What your competitors have that you do not,** and the pattern the top three share. It is usually unglamorous and repeatable, which is good news.
- **The fixes, ranked,** split into this week, this month, and this quarter.
- **An honest timeline** of what to expect, month by month.

**$79. Three business days. One PDF about your specific business, in your specific city.**

> What nobody can promise you, including me: a specific position, a specific traffic number, or a timeline that Google honors. Anybody promising those is guessing, and guessing confidently is not the same as knowing. Local search is mostly unglamorous consistency work, and the audit says so out loud, because that is the expectation that keeps you from quitting in week three.

## Notes and assumptions

- **Priced at $79.** Deliberately below the Website Teardown, because it is a narrower scope and a slightly faster job. It is also the easiest first purchase for a local service business, which makes it a good top-of-funnel service.
- **Time budget is 60 to 90 minutes,** broken down in the internal notes.
- **The most important operational rule is in the internal notes:** set the search location manually in an incognito window. Auditing local rankings from your own location is the single most common way these audits end up wrong, and it would be an embarrassing thing to get caught on.
- **If the client has no Google Business Profile at all, that is the whole audit.** The template says to stop there, fix that, and offer to re-run in 60 days. Charging $79 to produce twelve rows of "not applicable" is how you lose a customer permanently.
- Never promises a ranking position. Stated twice, once in the deliverable and once on the offer page.
- Natural bundle: the Mini-Audit plus the Google Review Booster Kit at $99, which is a discount on the pair and puts the tool for the biggest gap directly in the client's hands.
- Best upsell path: Mini-Audit, then Review Kit, then a monthly local visibility retainer or Website Creation.
