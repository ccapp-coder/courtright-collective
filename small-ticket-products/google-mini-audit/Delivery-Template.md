## 8. What to expect, honestly

Local search moves slowly. Here is a realistic shape rather than a promise:

- **Weeks 1 to 2:** profile fixes are live. Little visible change yet
- **Weeks 3 to 6:** the map pack usually starts responding to profile completeness and photo activity
- **Months 2 to 4:** review volume and consistency work start to show
- **Months 4 to 6:** the compounding part, if you kept it up

**What nobody can promise you, including me:** a specific position, a specific traffic number, or a timeline that Google honors. Anybody who does promise those is guessing, and guessing confidently is not the same as knowing.

## Internal notes, delete before sending

**Time budget: 60 to 90 minutes.**

- 15 min: run the searches. Use an incognito window and set the search location to their city, otherwise your own location skews everything
- 20 min: Google Business Profile review, competitor profiles too
- 10 min: review comparison
- 15 min: NAP consistency across the listed sites
- 15 min: website checks, run PageSpeed and a `site:` search
- 15 min: write it up

**Rules:**

- Set the search location manually. Auditing from the wrong city produces confidently wrong results
- Never promise a ranking position. Ever
- The review section is usually the biggest gap and the easiest sell for the Review Booster Kit
- If they do not have a Google Business Profile at all, that is the entire audit. Say so, fix that, and offer to run the audit again in 60 days
- Local SEO is mostly unglamorous consistency work. Say that out loud, because it sets expectations that keep the client happy at week three
