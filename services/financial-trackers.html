What We Actually Do

CFO-grade visibility, on autopilot.

You don't have a CFO. You probably don't have a bookkeeper checking in weekly either. We build the layer that watches your money for you, so "how's business?" gets a number instead of a shrug.

Live P&L

Revenue, expenses, gross margin, net profit. Updated daily from your accounting software, bank feeds, and payment processors. One dashboard, and you know how the month is shaping up.

Cashflow forecast

A 13-week rolling forecast built from your recurring revenue, scheduled bills, and history. Know whether you can afford to hire, invest, or take a draw. Three months before you have to guess.

Automatic expense categorization

The AI categorizes every transaction using your own categories and reviews the uncertain ones with you weekly. No more "uncategorized" pile waiting for you at year-end.

Invoice chasing

Outstanding invoices get gentle automated follow-ups written in your tone. It escalates if needed and stops the moment payment lands. You get paid without playing bad cop.

Tax-ready exports

At tax time your accountant gets a clean export: P&L, expense categories, 1099 contractor totals. No scramble, no "let me dig through Stripe" emails.

Built on what you already use

Your bank, your accounting software, your payment processors. We wire into what's already there. You don't switch tools, you just stop babysitting them.

The bookkeeping guilt you carry around all year.
