Common Questions
Do I own the code?

Yes. All source code, designs, and assets are yours at the end of the engagement. We hand over the repository, deployment access, and documentation. If you ever bring development in-house or switch teams, the handoff is clean.

What stack do you build on?

Web apps: TypeScript, React and Next.js, Supabase or Postgres, Cloudflare Workers on the backend. Mobile: SwiftUI for iOS, Kotlin or React Native for Android depending on the project. We pick the stack for the job, not the other way around.

What happens after launch?

You can take it from there, keep us on a monthly maintenance retainer, or scope the next phase. Most clients keep us on for at least the first six months so we can fix the things real users find that QA didn't.

Can you take over a project that's already started?

Yes, with caveats. We do a code review first to see how much rework is needed. Sometimes it's faster and cheaper to start fresh than to inherit somebody else's framework choices. We'll tell you straight.

Not ready to book a call?

Drop your email and I'll reach out personally. Tell me about the idea, or don't. Either way, no pitch deck and no pressure.
