Common Questions
Do I own the site, or is it locked into your platform?

You own it. The code, the domain, the content, all yours. If you ever decide to leave, we hand you the full source files and help you migrate. No hostage-taking.

What about my existing domain?

We point it to the new site. You keep ownership at your existing registrar, email stays untouched, and most migrations are zero-downtime.

Can I edit pages myself if I want to?

Yes, we can set up a simple CMS if you want to make edits directly. But most clients find it easier to just message us. Small changes usually ship the same day.

How long until a custom site is live?

About 14 days for a typical 1 to 5 page small business site. Longer for custom integrations, e-commerce, or large content migrations. We quote timing in the proposal, and we hit it.

Not ready to book a call?

Drop your email and I'll reach out personally. Tell me nothing, or reply with your current site. Either way, no pressure and no pitch deck.
