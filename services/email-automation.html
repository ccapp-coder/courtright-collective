What We Actually Do

Your inbox stays where it is. The work stops being yours.

Gmail, Outlook, whatever you use. The AI lives in the background, drafts land in your drafts folder, triage happens through labels you already understand. Nothing about your day changes except the inbox stops running it.

Inbox triage that actually holds

Every incoming email gets routed: needs a reply today, schedule a follow-up, archive, escalate. Marketing junk auto-archives. Threads with action items get flagged. You open your inbox to a prioritized list instead of a landslide.

Replies drafted in your voice

Sales inquiries, support questions, scheduling back-and-forth. The AI drafts the reply, trained on your past sent mail so it sounds like you and not a robot. Five-minute replies become thirty-second reviews.

Follow-ups that never forget

Leads who went quiet get a gentle nudge at 3 days, 7 days, 14 days. The moment they reply, the sequence stops. No more deals slipping through the cracks because Tuesday got busy.

Thread summaries and context

Long threads get summarized automatically. When a client comes back after a month of silence, the AI hands you the history before you reply. No more scrolling to remember what you quoted.

Calendar and CRM wiring

Replies that involve scheduling create calendar events. Replies from leads update your CRM. The inbox stops being an island and starts feeding the rest of your business.

You stay in control

By default, nothing sends without your approval. Drafts wait in your drafts folder. For high-volume, low-risk stuff (FAQ replies, mostly), some clients turn on auto-send later. Your call, always.

What This Replaces

The hour or two a day you spend being your own assistant.

  • Manually sorting mail into "needs reply," "FYI," and "junk"
  • Re-reading the same email three times because you forgot what was in it
  • Typing the same kind of reply over and over
  • Forgetting to follow up on leads that went quiet
  • Hunting through threads for attachments and context

Most owners we work with lose 1 to 2 hours a day inside their inbox. This usually pays for itself in the first month.

Common Questions
Will the AI send emails without me approving them?

Not unless you explicitly opt in. By default the AI drafts replies into your Drafts folder and you review and hit send. Some clients later enable auto-send for high-volume, low-risk emails like FAQ replies, with confidence thresholds. Your call.

Will it sound like me, or like a chatbot?

We train it on your past sent emails so it picks up your tone, your phrases, your level of formality. Most clients say the drafts sound more like them than the emails they wrote themselves at 11pm.

What about privacy? Where does my email go?

We can run the AI inside your existing Google Workspace or Microsoft 365, so email never leaves your account. For more advanced setups we use providers with no-training data agreements. We walk through the options on the call.

What providers do you support, and how fast is it live?

Gmail, Google Workspace, Outlook, Microsoft 365, and custom IMAP. A basic triage and draft-reply setup goes live in 1 to 2 weeks. Add about a week per extra integration like CRM or calendar.
