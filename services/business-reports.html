What We Actually Do

The numbers come to you, not the other way around.

We connect to your data once (read-only), agree on what matters, and then the reports just... show up. On schedule, in plain English, with the stuff worth acting on up top.

Weekly sales report

Every Monday at 7am: revenue, top products, conversion rate, top channels, week-over-week comparison. In your inbox before your coffee's done, ready to act on.

Monthly business snapshot

Revenue, expenses, customer metrics, growth rate, AR and AP status. The kind of report you'd build for a board meeting, generated automatically every month.

Live KPI dashboard

A web dashboard showing your most important numbers in real time. Check it on your phone before a meeting, put it on a TV in the office, keep it open in a tab all day.

Anomaly alerts

The AI watches for the weird stuff. A 30 percent drop in conversion, a customer who hasn't ordered in 90 days, a vendor invoice way off. It pings you before it becomes a problem.

Custom client reports

If you bill clients with monthly performance reports (agencies, service providers), we build them once and they generate themselves every month. Each client gets their numbers, branded.

Built around your tools

Stripe, Shopify, your CRM, your accounting software, Google Sheets. If you can get data out of it, we can almost certainly report on it.

What This Replaces

The report-building ritual you never signed up for.

  • Exporting CSVs from Stripe, Shopify, and your CRM every Monday
  • Copy-pasting numbers into a Google Sheet named REPORT_final_v3
  • Rebuilding the same client report by hand every month
  • Finding out something dropped weeks after it dropped
  • Answering "how's business?" with a shrug

Most owners we work with spend 2 to 4 hours a week building reports they could be reading in five minutes. We give you those hours back.
