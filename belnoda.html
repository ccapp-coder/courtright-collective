What It Is

Most business software makes you move in. Belnoda lets you add a room.

Here's the thing about the all-in-one platforms: you pay for forty features to use four. And the single-purpose apps each keep their own copy of your customer list, so nothing agrees with anything. Belnoda splits the difference. One foundation, and rooms you switch on when a problem shows up.

One client database

Every room reads the same list. Add a customer once and booking, messaging, support, and your books all know who they are.

One login

No juggling six subscriptions and six passwords. You sign in once and everything you've turned on is just there.

Toggleable modules

A module is a room. Need booking? Flip it on. Season's over? Flip it off. Your data stays put either way.

Manual first, automatic when you're ready

The base gives you the by-hand version of the work. Each module gives you the automatic version of the same thing. Upgrade when the by-hand version starts eating your evenings.

The free foundation comes with every account: your client contacts, your business info, a jobs list with your prices, and a by-hand P&L sheet. That's a working back office on day one, before you've turned on a single room.
